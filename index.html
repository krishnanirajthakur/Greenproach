📊 Understanding Scope Emissions

Greenhouse gases are grouped into three scopes: Scope 1 is direct emissions (like fuel in your car), Scope 2 is indirect emissions from bought energy (like home electricity), and Scope 3 covers all other value chain emissions (like manufacturing the products you buy).

🌍 The Paris Agreement 2°C Target

The global community agreed to limit warming to well below 2°C (ideally 1.5°C) compared to pre-industrial levels. To meet this, the average carbon footprint per person globally must fall below 2.0 tonnes of CO2e annually by 2050.

🌳 Avoid, Reduce, and Offset

The carbon mitigation hierarchy is clear: First, Avoid unnecessary emissions (walk instead of drive). Second, Reduce emissions that cannot be avoided (use public transit). Third, Offset residual emissions (by funding global reforestation or renewable projects).
